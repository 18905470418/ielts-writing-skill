### CRI-044 · B8-REP-015 · 8 · report
- 题目：Many criminals re-offended after they have been punished. Why do some people continue to commit crimes after they have been punished, and what measures can be ...
- [topic] One major reason some offenders commit crimes again after being punished is the difficulty they face in finding stable employment.
- [explain] Individuals with a criminal record may be viewed negatively by employers, who can be reluctant to offer them job opportunities.
- [mechanism] Consequently, former prisoners may struggle to secure regular employment and earn a stable income, particularly if they lack professional qualifications or practical skills.
- [explain] Without a reliable source of income, some may experience serious financial difficulties and struggle to meet their basic needs.
- [mechanism] As a result, they may return to illegal activities as a means of earning money, increasing the likelihood of re-offending.

### CRI-045 · B8-REP-015 · 8 · report
- 题目：Many criminals re-offended after they have been punished. Why do some people continue to commit crimes after they have been punished, and what measures can be ...
- [topic] One effective way to reduce re-offending is to provide prisoners with education and vocational training before they are released.
- [example] Governments could offer courses in practical fields such as construction, mechanics or information technology, allowing offenders to develop skills that are valued in the labour market.
- [explain] Acquiring these skills and recognised qualifications could improve their employment prospects and make them more attractive to potential employers.
- [mechanism] After release, former prisoners would therefore have a greater chance of securing legitimate employment and earning a stable income.
- [linkback] This financial stability could reduce their dependence on illegal activities, ultimately lowering the likelihood of committing further crimes.
